Privacy Policy — Pal Runner 3D

Package: com.palrunner3d.app · Last updated: November 16, 2026 · Contact: gkhatter28@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Pal Runner 3D ("the App") handles user data, local game progress, optional cloud synchronization, and third-party advertising SDKs on Android and Web.

1. Game Progress & Local Storage

By default, Pal Runner 3D works offline and stores your coins, unlocked runner skins, upgrade levels, hoverboard shields, audio/graphics settings, and ad consent preferences locally on your device. No account sign-in is required to play.

2. Optional Web Account Sign-In & Cloud Sync (Firebase)

If you voluntarily sign in with Google on the web version of Pal Runner 3D, Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore process:

  • Your game progress (/saves) to sync scores, coins, and unlocked items across devices.
  • Your verified email address and privacy consent flag in an isolated private record (/users_private) readable only by your account.
  • Your public display name, high score, best distance, equipped outfit, and shareable Friend Code on the Global & Friends Leaderboards (/leaderboard). Your email address is never exposed publicly.

3. Third-Party Advertising SDKs (Google AdMob & Unity Ads)

Pal Runner 3D displays optional rewarded video ads, banner ads, and periodic between-run interstitial ads on Android using:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Ads SDK (Unity Technologies) — Unity Privacy Policy

Depending on your ad personalization preference, these advertising partners may collect and process device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID), IP address (for coarse location and fraud prevention), device model and OS version, ad impressions, clicks, and SDK diagnostic/crash performance signals. You can switch between personalized and non-personalized ads at any time inside Settings → Ads & Privacy, or disable ads inside the app using the in-game Coin Supply Store's Ad-Free Express Pass.

4. Permissions Used on Android

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to check connectivity, load advertisements, and fetch public global leaderboard rankings.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Used by Google AdMob and Unity Ads on Android 13+ in accordance with your personalized-ad setting.

5. Children's Privacy

Pal Runner 3D is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your region). Ad requests are configured with a general audience content rating.

6. Data Deletion & Your Choices

You may reset local progress and delete any linked cloud account data (/saves, /users_private, and /leaderboard) directly inside the app via Settings → Privacy Policy → Delete Data, or contact us at gkhatter28@gmail.com.
